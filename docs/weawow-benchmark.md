# Weawow Benchmark Notes for Talibenah

Source reviewed: Google Play listing for `com.weawow`.

## Strengths to Learn From

1. Weather-matched photography

Weawow uses beautiful weather-related photos that reflect current conditions. Talibenah should keep the scenic city background idea, but make it more local and travel-oriented: city landmarks, mountains, forests, coastlines, old towns, and historical places.

2. No ads, donation-supported

Users repeatedly praise the lack of pop-up ads and banners. Talibenah should stay ad-free and use optional small support tiers instead of interruptive monetization.

3. Customizable layout

Weawow lets users choose what to show and sort details like temperature, rain, wind, gusts, pressure, UV, and more. Talibenah should add a "Customize home" screen where users can reorder metric cards and hide sections.

4. Changeable weather providers

Weawow supports multiple providers such as NWS, DWD, Meteo France, AEMET, MET Norway, OpenWeatherMap, and others. Talibenah should expose provider comparison clearly: selected source, backup source, freshness, and confidence.

5. Rich widgets

Widget customization is one of Weawow's strongest retention features. Talibenah should eventually include Android widgets for current weather, hourly forecast, daily forecast, clock + weather, and rain alert.

6. Notifications without noise

Weawow supports daily forecast notifications and ongoing weather notification. Talibenah should add calm notification modes: morning summary, rain starting soon, severe alert, and status-bar current temperature.

7. Privacy positioning

The listing highlights no ads and no data collection. Talibenah should make privacy visible: local-first preferences, minimal location handling, no tracker SDKs, and clear source attribution.

## Talibenah Differentiators

- Better Persian and Romanian localization from day one.
- Tourism-aware city backgrounds, not just weather photos.
- A mountain/nature identity rooted in Talibenah, Gilan.
- Multi-source forecast confidence, not just source switching.
- Local-currency support tiers with modest amounts.
- Radar and satellite map surfaces integrated into the forecast flow.
